# Working in this repo

- Decks live in `decks/<name>/` (blueprint.json + assets/); builds go to `decks/<name>/out/` (git-ignored).
- **Dog-training decks (משק דמתי · בית לכלבים):** every deck for the dog school must carry the school
  logo. Merge `brands/mishek-dmati/brand.json` into the blueprint (`design.logo` + the two `assets[]`
  entries) and copy the files it lists into the deck's `assets/`. Don't place the logo by hand.
